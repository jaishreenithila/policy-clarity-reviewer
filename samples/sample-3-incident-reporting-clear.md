# Sample 3: AI incident reporting (mostly clear, control sample)

Use: a well-written policy section. Expect few or no findings. This checks that the reviewer does not invent problems just to fill the table.

---

**AI Incident Reporting**

Scope. This section applies to every employee and contractor who uses an approved artificial intelligence (AI) tool for company work.

Definition. An AI incident is any event where an AI tool produces output that exposes personal data, discriminates against a person, or causes a financial loss over £1,000.

Requirements.

1. If you find an AI incident, you must report it to the AI Governance Lead within 24 hours.
2. You must report it through the AI Incident Form on the intranet.
3. You must not delete the prompt or the output until the AI Governance Lead closes the incident.

Responsibilities.

- The AI Governance Lead must acknowledge each report within 2 working days.
- The AI Governance Lead must record each incident in the AI Risk Register.
- The Data Protection Officer must decide within 72 hours whether a personal data incident needs reporting to the regulator.

Review. The AI Governance Lead must review this section every 12 months.
