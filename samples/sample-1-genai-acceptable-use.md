# Sample 1: Generative AI acceptable use (deliberately unclear)

Use: a typical first draft with many clarity problems. Expect a long table.

---

**Generative AI Acceptable Use Policy**

1. Purpose. It should be noted that the company values responsible innovation and this policy is intended to support the appropriate use of GenAI tools in order to drive productivity while managing risk.

2. Staff should try to only use approved tools. A list of approved AI systems will be maintained and updated periodically.

3. Confidential information and PII must not be entered into public models unless it is necessary for the task and appropriate safeguards are in place, or the relevant manager has agreed, and the data has been anonymised where possible.

4. Outputs from AI tools must be reviewed before being shared externally. Significant errors should be reported in a timely manner.

5. Use of LLMs for decisions about employees, such as hiring, promotion, performance, etc., is prohibited except where approved.

6. Breaches of this policy will be dealt with as needed.
